The most explicit realization of local class field theory we have
encountered for the case of cyclotomic fields over the field Qp, i.e., with the
extensions Qp(f )|Qp, where f is a pn-th root of unity. The notion of formal

"division points" that do the same for the field K as the pn-th roots of unity
do for the field Qp.
