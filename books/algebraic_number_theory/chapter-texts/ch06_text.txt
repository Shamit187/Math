base field is taken in global class field theory by the idele class group. The

analogous problems for the various completions Kp. Chevalley used the term
"ideal element", which was abbreviated as id. el.

§ 7. The Ideal-Theoretic Version of Class Field Theory
Class field theory has found its idele-theoretic formulation only after it
