Slow Books

Math and statistics textbooks rush. These companion guides slow them down — every definition unpacked, every step spelled out, with read-aloud TTS and comments on any paragraph.

What this is

A site served locally that walks through dense textbooks and research papers at the pace they should have been written. Every paragraph has a comment button — leave notes on anything that doesn't land, and the explanation gets rewritten in response. A TTS player reads blocks aloud at adjustable speed.
