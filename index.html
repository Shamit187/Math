Slow Books

Math and statistics textbooks rush. These companion guides slow them down — every definition unpacked, every "obvious" step spelled out, with comments you can leave on any paragraph.

What this is

A small static site, served locally, that walks chapter by chapter through dense textbooks at the pace they should have been written. Every paragraph, list, and callout box has a comment button — leave notes on what didn't make sense, and the explainer for that block can be rewritten in response.

How it works

  1. Pick a book below.
  2. Read the chapter pages. Hover any block to reveal a 💬 button (or press C).
  3. Comments persist to comments/comments.json. They survive reloads, theme changes, and re-edits of the prose.
  4. When a section is unclear, leave a comment. The next pass at that block will be rewritten with your feedback in hand.
